# Bubble - Mindful Pen Pal & Thought Exchange

An intentional, minimalist pen pal and thought exchange web application for introverts and mindful thinkers, inspired by Slowly's philosophy. Built with customizable 2D vector illustrated avatars, weekly thought bubbles drifting across air currents, aggregate burst limits (3 of 5 daily bubbles), breath mechanics, mutual media handshakes, and shared hobby discovery without superficial swipe dynamics or dating cringe.

## User Decisions & Product Alignment

- **Platform Pivot**: Pure friend-finding, craft, and intellectual thought exchange across locations. No cheesy romance, dating solicitation, or lookism.
- **Bubble Metaphor (No Letters, No Stamps)**: Public posts are floating bubbles with unique aura hues and breath energies. Opening a thought is bursting the bubble.
- **Aggregate Daily Burst Quota**: Top header displays aggregate status: "3 Bursts Available · From 5 Daily Bubbles". Cards stay minimal and uncluttered.
- **Weekly Release Quota**: 1 bubble released per week per user, ensuring each thought is composed with intentionality.
- **Thought Sparks**: Slowly-style inspirational writing prompts to eliminate writer's block and motivate intellectual depth.
- **Content Moderation & Cleanse Service**:
  - **Strict Title Word Limit (10 words)**: Easy to scan, preventing spam or solicitation hooks.
  - **Title Cleanse Action**: Offending cringe/dating words are stripped from the title at all costs, with a live `⚡ Cleanse Title` one-click fix.
  - **Live Content Rephrasing Guidance**: Real-time advice helping users rephrase thoughts away from romance/dating tropes toward books, crafts, and ideas.
  - **Asynchronous Deep Content Review**: Bubbles with good titles enter public airspace immediately; their contents are processed asynchronously in the background. If flagged, the bubble is immediately brought down.
  - **Three Warnings Rule & 7-Day Suspension**: After 3 moderation warnings, the account is suspended for 7 days. Releasing bubbles, bursting bubbles, and dispatches are paused with a suspension countdown banner (and a dev test reset control).
- **Zero Public Media**: Public bubbles strictly forbid photos, audio, video, or hyperlinks.
- **1-on-1 Mutual Media Handshake**: Media (voice notes, photos, video clips) is strictly unlocked after bilateral 2-way approval between connected friends.
- **50 Connections Capacity & Stealth Removal**: Maximum 50 friends; stealth removal silently frees slots without notifying the other party.
- **Paced Air Current Delivery**: Calm transit delays (10–25 minutes based on distance/air currents) with a fast-forward tester tool.
